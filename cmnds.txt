Monitoring URLs (Run these in separate terminals):

1. Grafana (Dashboards)
Command: kubectl port-forward svc/kube-prometheus-grafana 3000:80 -n observability
URL: http://localhost:3000
Credentials: admin / admin123

2. Prometheus (Raw Metrics)
Command: kubectl port-forward svc/kube-prometheus-kube-prome-prometheus 9090:9090 -n observability
URL: http://localhost:9090
Query Example: messages_sent_total
