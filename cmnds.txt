2. Open Prometheus (Raw Metrics)
Use this if you want to run raw queries (PromQL) to see your custom app metrics.

kubectl port-forward svc/kube-prometheus-kube-prome-prometheus 9090:9090 -n observability
Open your browser to: http://localhost:9090
Query Example: Type messages_sent_total and click Execute to see your app's data

query : messages_sent_total
